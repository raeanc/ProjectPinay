Project Pinay

According to the United Nations Population Fund, 1 in 10 Filipino girls aged 15-19 are already mothers or expecting. We want to change things.

WHO ARE WE?

I want to read this site in Filipino!/Gusto kong basahin ito sa Filipino!

Project Pinay is an international rotaract initiative under Rotaract Club of Singapore that focuses on educating youths in the Philippines on mental, puberty & sexual education, in hopes of helping them make more informed choices.

OUR MISSION

To educate & empower Filipino youths by providing accessible mental & sexual education, puberty awareness, & menstrual hygiene knowledge through interactive lessons & community engagement.

WHY "PINAY"?

The term "Pinay" (pee-nai) is a colloquial term used to refer to a Filipino woman or girl, which is what Project Pinay is all about, empowering Filipino girls & women!

WHY THE PHILIPPINES?

Teenage pregnancy in the Philippines remains alarmingly high, with UNFPA echoing NEDA & POPCOM in describing it as a "national emergency.", citing how only 28% of Filipino adolescents have access to comprehensive sexuality education in schools.
